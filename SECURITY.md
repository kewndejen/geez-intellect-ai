Reporting a Vulnerability

Should you discover a fissure in our digital fortifications or a potential vulnerability within the AI Studio, we request a private disclosure to ensure the stability of the Sovereign network.
How to Report

Please do not open a public GitHub issue for security vulnerabilities.

    Private Disclosure: Send a detailed technical report to the Grand Architect’s team via email at: [deaconkewndejen@gmail.com]

    Details Requested:

        A clear description of the vulnerability.

        Step-by-step instructions to reproduce the issue (Proof of Concept).

        Potential impact on the system or research data.

Our Commitment

Once a report is received, GE'EZ STUDIO will act with the following rigor:

    Acknowledgement: You will receive a formal receipt of your report within 48 hours.

    Triage & Validation: Our technical scribes will validate the finding within 5 business days.

    Resolution: If a vulnerability is confirmed, we will develop a patch and provide a timeline for deployment.

    Scholarly Recognition: Contributors who discover significant vulnerabilities will be formally acknowledged in our "Sovereign Security Contributors" log, preserving their name in the project’s history (unless anonymity is preferred).

Security Best Practices

    Secrets Management: Never hard-code API keys (Google AI Studio Keys) in the repository. Always use Streamlit Secrets or Environment Variables.

    Data Integrity: The AI is designed for research; ensure uploaded manuscripts do not contain sensitive personal data.
